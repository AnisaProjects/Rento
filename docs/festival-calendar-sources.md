Collected 2026-09-27. Loaded into `tblfestivals` by `database/migrations/008_festivals.sql`; edit the dates in admin -> Festivals.


Researched 2026-09-27. BS 2083 dates come from the official MoHA Nepal Gazette (Khanda 75, Sankhya 67, 2082-11-18; PDF scanned pages read directly) and are cross-checked. **BS 2084 is not official yet.** MoHA has not gazetted the 2084 holiday list, and the Nepal Panchang Nirnayak Vikas Samiti is still collecting 2084 calendar submissions (deadline Asoj 20, 2083 ≈ 2026-10-06, per Onlinekhabar). All 2084 dates below come from independent panchang calendars (ashesh.com.np, drikpanchang for Kathmandu) and should be re-checked when the official 2084 list comes out (normally Falgun, i.e. Feb/Mar 2027).

Events already past on 2026-09-27 are left out: Indra Jatra 2026-09-25, Constitution Day 2026-09-19, Teej 2026-09-14. Minor 2026 items also left out: Jitiya 2026-10-04 (women employees only, Asoj 18), Falgunanda Jayanti 2026-11-11, Guru Nanak Jayanti 2026-11-24, Disability Day 2026-12-03, Martyrs' Day 2027-01-30, Women's Day 2027-03-08.
