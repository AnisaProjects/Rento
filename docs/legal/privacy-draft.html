2. Why we use it

  • To handle your account, booking requests and enquiries, and to contact you about them (performing our contract with you).

  • To send emails you asked for or that belong to your booking: password reset links, "booking request received", "booking confirmed" and "request received".
  • To send the newsletter, only if you subscribed. To unsubscribe, contact us and we remove your address. [TO CONFIRM: add an unsubscribe link to newsletter emails.]

3. Smart pricing and recommendations

  • Our smart pricing sets daily prices from the calendar (weekends, festivals, seasons) and, once there are enough bookings, how busy each vehicle is. It uses booking counts, not information about you: two people see the same price for the same car and dates.
  • "Find My Car" suggests vehicles from what you type (trip, budget, travellers). If you are logged in, it also takes into account the types and brands you rented before. What you type is used for that search only and is not stored with your account.
