## F.3 Observations

- Almost every operator rents with a driver; Self Drive Nepal was the only one that published self-drive prices, and it requires a large security deposit and a driving licence held for three years.
- Most operators take bookings through enquiry forms, telephone, WhatsApp or Viber, and few publish fixed prices for tours; only Sajilo Rental offered instant booking with online payment (eSewa, Khalti and cards).
- Frequent extras are the driver's allowance of Rs 1,500–2,500 per night, 13% VAT on request, night surcharges of 25–50%, surcharges for hill or gravel roads, one-way drop fees and an informal peak-season increase of 10–15% in October and November.
- No operator showed a price for a particular day, explained a price difference, or recommended vehicles for a described trip.
